Using This References Section

This section provides traceability between the book's content and its sources. When reading a chapter:

  1. Check the chapter's "Sources" section for the works that chapter draws on
  2. Follow the Source [N] link to the numbered entry above for full bibliographic details
  3. Use the "Referenced in" lists to find related discussions across chapters

Every numbered citation in the manuscript resolves to exactly one entry in the numbered source index, and every "Referenced in" list is generated from the chapters that actually cite the source (verified with scripts/audit_references.py on 30 October 2026). Where two source numbers point to the same document, the entries say so. Bibliographic details (titles, editions, years, ISBNs and URLs) were checked against publishers, standards bodies and the cited web pages during the fact-check of October 2026; URLs were last accessed on 30 October 2026. Chapter Sources sections may additionally list a small number of verified works without a source number; these are described in full where they appear.
